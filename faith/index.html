OUR PRINCIPLES

What we stand on

  • Love God. With all our heart, soul, and mind.
  • Love people. Our neighbors in La Porte and everywhere else.
  • Tell the truth. Honest forecasts, real sources, no hype.
  • Be prepared. “A prudent man foreseeth the evil, and hideth himself” (Proverbs 22:3).
  • Care for all that lives. God put us in the garden “to dress it and to keep it” (Genesis 2:15).
