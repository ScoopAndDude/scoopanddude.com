WEATHER & YOUR WALLET

Why a weather site watches the markets

  • Heating bills: natural gas prices climb when a cold winter is forecast. It's the fuel behind most NIPSCO heating bills.
  • Crops: corn and soybeans are Indiana's biggest crops, and their prices swing with rain, drought and heat in the Midwest.
  • Gas at the pump: hurricanes in the Gulf can shut refineries and raise prices here within days.
  • El Niño: a strong one, like this year's, changes growing seasons worldwide. Follow it on our El Niño watch.
